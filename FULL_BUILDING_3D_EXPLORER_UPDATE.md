# ProofMatch AI — Full Building 3D Explorer Update

This update expands **Inside the Company** into a fuller building-exploration experience.

## Highlights
- Company explorer now represents **Company X Tower**.
- Expanded from a small two-floor experience into a **6-floor office building**.
- Added a **building overview** panel and **floor directory** for quick navigation.
- Floors now cover: Atrium, Engineering & Product, Business & Operations, People & Learning, Innovation & Sustainability, and Leadership & Partner Network.
- Each floor includes multiple interactive stations tied to careers, departments, and ProofMatch AI opportunity pathways.

- `/demo/career/company`
