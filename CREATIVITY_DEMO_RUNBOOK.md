# ProofMatch AI — Creativity Upgrade Demonstration Runbook

All sample candidate information is fictional. Reviewers decide; the prototype does not hire anyone.

## 100–130-second showcase

| Time | Screen | Action and claim |
|---|---|---|
| 0–12 s | `/` | Start with premium editorial landing page, click **Explore ProofMatch**. The two-panel **Door to Opportunity** opens into the workspace; shortcut **Skip intro** is available. |
| 12–35 s | `/demo/discover` | Select **Candidate 01** and reveal their better-evidenced alternative to Frontend: **QA Automation Engineer**. Explain that this follows the supplied sample's pytest and CI excerpts. |
| 35–55 s | `/demo/matches?candidate=C01&role=qa` | Click **Automated testing** in **Evidence X-Ray** and reveal the exact fictional source quote. Emphasize supporting information is not proof of real skill. |
| 55–90 s | `/demo/fairshot?candidate=C01&role=qa&skill=api_testing` | Show the predefined duplicate-order API scenario. Enter a fictional response of 35+ characters. Save it and explain that it remains a candidate statement awaiting review. Optionally request a Gemini follow-up when the key works. |
| 90–108 s | `/demo/matches?candidate=C01&role=qa` | Show the self-reported additional evidence and click **Save for HR review**. Human decision stays in control. |
| 108–130 s | `/demo/proof-over-polish` | Briefly run the controlled wording comparison. Be explicit that this is only a rules-engine stability check, **not** a fairness benchmark. |

## Backup and failure plans

- Door animation bypassed? Use **Try the demo** directly; reduced-motion settings intentionally skip it.
- Gemini quota or network error? The deterministic matching works without Gemini. The FairShot follow-up clearly labels its prewritten fallback. Do not call prewritten guidance a live AI result.
- Microphone or WebGL unsupported? Use Mira's typed chat; core matching never depends on avatar rendering.
- Browser data cleared? Recreate the fictional FairShot answer; the seed candidate and job roles are bundled.
- Do not show client private data. Do not claim candidate responses are verified or recruitment outcomes improve without evaluation.
